Methodology & interpretation

Population records are joined to Census EA polygons using a shared EA identifier where available. The application tests common fields such as feacode, FEA_Code, EA_code and Join_ID. Age-group totals are calculated from the workbook records and are not estimated. Unmatched records are reported in the join diagnostics. Results depend on the source workbook's geographic coverage and definitions; do not interpret missing values as zero population.
