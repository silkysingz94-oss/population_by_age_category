Interpretation & methodology

The Excel workbook is treated as an independent settlement-level population table. Population values are summed across the selected records. The GeoJSON is treated as an independent Enumeration Area boundary dataset and is displayed without settlement population values. Because the supplied datasets do not share a common geographic identifier, the application deliberately performs no spatial or attribute join. Settlement-code repetitions across wards are retained as separate source records.
