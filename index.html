12 • Methodology and data
Geographic source: Census_EAs_Geo.json supplied by the user.

Weather source: Open-Meteo forecast API.

Spatial method: Enumeration Area polygon centroid is used as the representative weather coordinate. A centroid is not necessarily a settlement or meteorological station location.

Forecast interpretation: Weather conditions are model-based estimates and should not be interpreted as ground observations or official Gambia Meteorological Services forecasts.
